2nd half, 2022

  • Secured $40K Concordium grant, validating the idea.
  • Ukraine dev team disrupted by war, causing execution issues.
  • Lack of experience in hiring & execution slowed progress.

Survival & Product Development

  • Grant ended → Bootstrapped again and set up legal in Mexico.
